⚙️ Procedure
🔹 Part A: Enumeration
Step 1: Service Enumeration
nmap -sV scanme.nmap.org

👉 Identifies:

Open ports
Service versions
Step 2: Detailed Enumeration
nmap -A scanme.nmap.org

👉 Extracts:

OS details
Running services
Scripts output
Step 3: DNS Enumeration
nmap --script dns-brute example.com

👉 Finds:

Subdomains
Step 4: SMB/User Enumeration (Local Network Only)
nmap --script smb-enum-users <target-ip>

👉 Retrieves:

User accounts
Shared resources
